Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Your privacy is critically important. Soundia is designed with a privacy-first mindset:

  • Data Collection: Soundia does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, account information, or usage analytics.
  • Audio Processing: All spatial audio generation via AVAudioEngine happens entirely locally on your physical device.
  • Third Parties: The app does not integrate with any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or external tracking tools.
  • Sound Source: The sounds are from free open source.

If you have any questions regarding this policy, please use the support contact button above.
